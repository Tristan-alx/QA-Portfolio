# Killphrase — Playtest Report

**Developer:** J.N.01
**Platform:** Steam (demo)
**Genre:** Typing-based roguelite
**Tester:** Loïc Alexandre (Wattmole)

## Summary
Played through the demo build focusing on the level-selection flow, the two typing-quirk mechanics, the shop between levels, and every enemy type encountered. Reported UI clarity issues on the level-select screen and a few gameplay/balance observations on enemy variety and boss difficulty.

## Issues Found

| # | Severity | Area | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low | UX | Floor-progression indicator on the left of the level-select screen reads as pure decoration; its informational purpose was not discovered until the final run. |
| 2 | Low | UX / Accessibility | Color-coded pickup information (yellow = scrap, purple = EMP, etc.) is hard to distinguish at a glance even though each color maps to a specific meaning. |
| 3 | Low | UX | The input legend under level selection is unclear — the "L / Packet Loss" option had no perceivable effect during testing. |
| 4 | Medium | Balance | The "tank/roomba" enemy's typing pattern (e.g. `WSX EDC RFV TGB`) is a simple top-to-bottom sequence; once recognized, it stops feeling engaging. |
| 5 | Medium | Balance | The floor 10 boss is noticeably less threatening than the laser-based boss on Medium difficulty, undercutting its role as a final encounter. |

## Positive Notes
- Pixel art praised specifically for the merchant NPC and end-of-demo NPC.
- The two typing-quirk mechanics per level are engaging and well designed.
- Wheel enemies blocked by corpse collision create effective tension.
- Chaser-type robot enemies (compared to Resident Evil's Nemesis) were a standout, especially combined with the mid-screen speed-boost modifier.
- Image-based "guess the correct word" enemy was well received; player wanted to see it more often.
- Strong attention to polish: contextual enemy barks, multi-hit-bar enemies showing damage state, screen shake on heavy enemies.

## Developer Response
Developer confirmed the level-select screen would be cleaned up, and that the ASCII descent marker was unclear decoration to be reworked. Confirmed plans for a "plants and mushrooms" word-mixing gimmick already in concept art. Acknowledged the floor 10 boss needs tuning and higher difficulty tiers are planned. More enemy variety confirmed for deeper floors.
