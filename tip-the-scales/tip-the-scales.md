# Tip the Scales — Playtest Report

**Developer:** benjaminadams
**Platform:** itch.io
**Genre:** Rhythm game (3-day game jam entry)
**Tester:** Loïc Alexandre (Wattmole)

## Summary
Short playtest of a game-jam rhythm title. Feedback focused on the clarity of the core timing mechanic, since the jam-scope constraints were understood going in.

## Issues Found

| # | Severity | Area | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | Core mechanic | Click timing feedback is unclear — no perceivable penalty for clicking too early or spamming, and no clear reward for landing a precise hit. On Very Hard, this made it ambiguous whether double-clicking was required when two food items overlapped horizontally. |
| 2 | Low | Audio | Music loops after ~40 seconds, understood as a likely licensing/scope constraint of the jam rather than an oversight. |
| 3 | Low | Polish | After a song ends, participants queue at the weight machine; the first one to sit causes later participants to visibly walk over already-seated participants to reach their spot. |

## Positive Notes
- The character-stretch visual gag on a high score was called out as the highlight and a satisfying reward.
- The 5 character models and their animations each felt distinct and fit their role.

## Developer Response
Developer clarified the game was built in 3 days for a jam without expecting full polish, and described the feedback as very constructive despite the informal framing.
