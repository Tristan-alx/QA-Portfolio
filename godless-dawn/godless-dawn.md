# Godless Dawn — Playtest Report

**Developer:** Celal Şahin (Hawk Studio Entertainment)

**Platform:** Studio website form (hawkstudioent.com), structured checklist + optional written comments

**Genre:** Souls-like (pre-alpha, 3 testable bosses)

**Tester:** Loïc Alexandre

## Summary
Playtest of an early pre-alpha build limited to 3 boss encounters, discovered through
the PlayTester Discord community and conducted via a structured feedback form on the
developer's own website (checklist-based categories plus optional written comments)
rather than free-form written reports. The developer, working solo, sent testers a
generic build brief ahead of testing specifying the areas of focus and known
limitations (see below). Feedback was submitted directly through the form; the
submitted checklist responses are not retained on my end.

## Developer Brief (generic notice sent to all testers before testing)
The developer flagged the build as an early test focused on gathering feedback on
bosses, combat, and overall gameplay feel rather than final quality, and noted that
SFX/audio and UI were incomplete or placeholder at this stage. Testers were asked to
focus specifically on:
- Boss fights
- Combat feel
- Difficulty and balancing
- Controls and responsiveness
- Bugs or unexpected behaviour
- Anything frustrating, unclear, or not fun

## Notes
This session reflects experience with structured/categorized test protocols, distinct
from the free-form written reports in the rest of this portfolio. No developer response
to the submitted feedback specifically has been received to date.
