# Buddy and Friends — Playtest Report

**Developer:** Alexander Wiseman

**Platform:** itch.io

**Genre:** Atmospheric horror

**Tester:** Loïc Alexandre (Wattmole)

## Summary
Playtest covering the early cave/forest sections. Testing ended before completion due to a progression blocker on a jump sequence, reported below alongside a soft-lock bug.

## Issues Found

| # | Severity | Area | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Blocking | Dying near the saw blades by the spray (including before the spray's animation is triggered) can leave the player unable to move or interact at all after respawning. |
| 2 | Medium | Trigger logic | If the wolf has not yet been encountered, dragging the meat toward it does not trigger its dialogue until the meat is dragged further out of frame, past a specific landmark, the trigger zone doesn't match player expectations. |
| 3 | Medium | Progression | The return jump using the spray could not be completed after 3 attempts, with each failed attempt requiring a full restart. This blocked further progress and ended the session. |
| 4 | Low | Text | A dialogue line ("The things that are real are not our...") reads as missing a word. |

## Positive Notes
- Background art (forest and cave) and the unsettling lullaby-style music were called out as effectively establishing atmosphere early on.
- The cat's introduction and its animation/music pairing were a highlight.

## Notes
My session ended without reaching the rest of the game due to the jump-sequence blocker above; I intent to revisit once further along in development.
